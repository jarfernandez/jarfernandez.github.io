Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026

José Arturo Fernández Díaz built WiFi Intruder Detector as a free app. This page explains what information the app uses, why, and where it is kept.

In short: the app does not collect any personal information. Everything it stores stays on your device and is never sent to the developer or to third parties.

What the app does

The app scans the Wi-Fi network your device is connected to, lists the devices that answer, and can alert you when a new device appears. It only sends light requests to addresses on that local network. It does not try to access, block or interfere with other devices.

Information stored on your device

To work, the app stores the following information on your device only:

  • The networks you scan: Wi-Fi name, address range and router.
  • The devices found: IP address, the names and types that devices announce on the network, and the manufacturer when it is known.
  • The names, icons and trust status you set for devices.
  • Scan history and device events.
  • Your settings, such as theme, language and new-device alerts.

This information is never sent to the developer or to third parties. It is excluded from cloud backups and from device-to-device transfers, and it is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data. The app cannot see the MAC addresses of other devices.

Permissions

The app asks only for what it needs to scan the Wi-Fi network you are connected to:

  • Internet access (INTERNET): to send light requests to devices on your local network. The app does not use it to contact any server of its own.
  • Network and Wi-Fi state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE, ACCESS_WIFI_STATE): to know whether you are on Wi-Fi and to read the network's address range and router.
  • Multicast (CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE): to receive the names that devices announce on the local network.
  • Local network access (ACCESS_LOCAL_NETWORK, Android 17 and later): required by Android to reach devices on your local network.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), optional: Android only shows the Wi-Fi name to apps with location access. The app uses it only to show the network name. It never reads, stores or shares your location.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS, Android 13 and later), optional: to alert you when a scan finds a new device and to show scan progress.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): to let a scan you started finish if you leave the app, with a visible notification you can cancel.

You can deny the optional permissions; the app keeps working with fewer details.

Third-party services

The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting services. It is distributed through Google Play, and Google Play may show a rating prompt inside the app. That prompt is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

Children's privacy

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this privacy policy

This policy may be updated, for example if new features change how information is handled. Changes are posted on this page with a new effective date.
